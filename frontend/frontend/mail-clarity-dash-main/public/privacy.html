AIMail Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

AIMail helps you answer and write work email. It reads the email in the Gmail account you connect, hides personal details before any text is sent to an AI model, writes reply drafts and new emails from your brief, and sends nothing until you approve it.

AIMail is a final-year project by Group MDS25 at Monash University Malaysia. This policy covers the AIMail web app at https://ai-mail-nu.vercel.app and the AIMail Chrome extension.

What AIMail keeps

  • Your account. Your name and email address from Google sign-in, your role and responsibilities if you enter them, and your settings (writing style, saved replies, quiet hours, rules, Private mode and other preferences).
  • Your Gmail connection. A Google refresh token so AIMail can read and send mail for you. It is stored encrypted.
  • Your email, masked. For each email: the subject, the body and a short preview, with personal details replaced by placeholders such as [PERSON_1] or [PHONE_1]. Names, email addresses, phone numbers, Malaysian IC numbers, card numbers and some account numbers are hidden. Some details are not hidden: company names, street numbers, and account numbers with nothing around them to show what they are. The sender's email address is kept as it is, because AIMail needs it to send your reply. AIMail also keeps the draft it wrote, a summary, action items, a priority and a category.
  • The hidden details, locked. The real values behind the placeholders are kept in an encrypted vault for each email, so AIMail can show them to you and fill them into your reply when it is sent. They are never sent to an AI model.
  • Replies you send. A masked copy of each reply you send, so AIMail can show which ones are still waiting for an answer. If you switch on "Learn from the replies I send", AIMail also keeps the draft it showed you next to what you sent, to learn your style.
  • New emails you write. For each email you start in Compose: the recipients' addresses as you typed them (needed to send it), the subject, your brief and the email, the documents it was grounded on and the checks it passed. Email addresses, phone, IC, passport and card numbers you type are kept as placeholders, with their values in an encrypted vault for that email, and filled in when you send.
  • Documents you add. Policies or other documents you upload as background for replies and new emails. They are masked before they are stored.
  • Audit records. A log of actions such as drafting, refining, sending, disconnecting and deleting. It records what happened and when, with an internal user id; it holds no email content. It cannot be edited or deleted, so tampering can be detected.
  • Egress records. For each prompt sent to an AI model: what it was for, which model provider, how many characters, a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the text, and how many details were hidden. The text itself is not kept. These records are what the "Privacy receipt" in AIMail shows.

What goes to Google Gemini

By default AIMail uses Google Gemini to read, summarise and draft replies. Only masked text is sent: the email and earlier messages in the thread with placeholders in place of personal details, matching passages from your documents (also masked), and your writing style. The vault of real details is never sent.

Exceptions you should know about:

  • Scanned attachments. By default a scan is read on AIMail's own servers and its image is not sent to Gemini. If you choose "checked" scans, a local model first looks for signatures, faces and stamps, and only an image where it finds none is sent to Gemini, with the text it could read redacted. The local check misses about 1 in 10 signatures, faces and stamps, and text the local reader could not see is not redacted.
  • What you type yourself. When you refine a draft, edit it, search or ask a question, AIMail hides fixed-format details (email addresses, phone numbers, IC numbers, card numbers), puts details it already knows back into placeholders, and hides any other name it finds. Some names, especially Malay or Chinese names written without forms such as bin, binti, a/l or a Chinese surname, can still be missed and sent as you typed them. If the name check cannot run, AIMail refuses the request rather than send the text.
  • Emails not in English. The masked subject and the sender's own text are translated into English once, so AIMail's other steps can read them. The translation is shown in the Privacy receipt and deleted with the email's text.
  • New emails. When AIMail writes or refines a new email, the model receives your brief, subject and draft with typed details as placeholders and names hidden, your recipients' names as placeholders, and matching passages from your documents. Never the recipients' addresses.
  • Search. To find matching passages, the masked text of your documents and emails is turned into numbers (embeddings) by Gemini, unless you use Private mode.

Google's handling of data sent to Gemini is governed by Google's terms for the Gemini API, not by AIMail.

The Chrome extension

The AIMail extension shows your AIMail summary, action items and draft beside Gmail.

  • It reads only the ID of the Gmail conversation you have open, from the page address and one page attribute. It never reads, copies or changes email content in Gmail.
  • It uses your existing AIMail sign-in. It stores no passwords or tokens.
  • Everything it shows comes from AIMail's servers under this policy, and it sends a reply only when you click Approve & Send.
  • Uninstalling it removes it completely. Your AIMail account and data are not affected; delete those from Settings in AIMail.

How AIMail uses Google user data

See AIMail and Google user data below for the Gmail permissions AIMail asks for, what each is used for, and AIMail's Limited Use commitment.

Private mode

If your organisation runs its own model, you can switch on Private mode in Settings. Your drafts, summaries, refines, translations and searches then use that model on your organisation's machine instead of Gemini. Masking still applies. Documents and emails already sent to Gemini for search before you switched stay sent.

Deleting your data

  • Disconnect Gmail (Settings > Account): AIMail stops reading your mailbox, revokes its access at Google, and deletes everything it stored from that mailbox: emails, sent replies, new emails you wrote, holding replies, egress records, and what it learned from your sends.
  • Delete your account (Settings > Account): everything above, plus your documents, writing style, saved replies, settings and your sign-in.
  • Audit records stay after deletion. They keep an internal id that no longer links to an account, and no email content.
  • Backups. A copy of the database is kept for recovery; only the newest 7 copies are kept, so deleted data can remain in a copy until it is replaced.
  • You can also remove AIMail's access yourself in your Google account, under Security > Third-party access. That stops access but does not delete what AIMail already stored.

What AIMail does not do

  • It does not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • It does not use your data for anything other than helping you read and answer your email.
  • It does not use your data to train AI models.

AIMail and Google user data

Part of the AIMail Privacy Policy. Last updated: 21 October 2026

AIMail signs you in with Google and, with your permission, connects to your Gmail account. This section explains exactly what AIMail asks Google for and why.

What AIMail asks Google for

PermissionWhat AIMail uses it for
Your name, email address and profile picture (Google sign-in)To create your AIMail account and show who is signed in.
Read your Gmail (gmail.readonly)To read new emails and their threads so AIMail can mask personal details, summarise them, list action items and draft replies. AIMail cannot delete, archive or change anything in your Gmail.
Send email as you (gmail.send)To send a reply or a new email you wrote only after you click Approve & Send, or a holding reply only if you switched that feature on.

How AIMail uses this data

  • Only to provide AIMail's features to you: reading, summarising, drafting and sending replies you approve.
  • Personal details are masked before any email text is stored or sent to an AI model (see the main policy).
  • The Gmail access token is stored encrypted and is never shown to you, other users or the AI model.
  • Disconnecting Gmail in Settings revokes AIMail's access at Google and deletes the mail AIMail stored.

Who it is shared with

  • Google Gemini, the AI model provider, receives masked text only, to produce summaries and drafts. In Private mode no email text goes to Gemini.
  • Supabase (database) and Render and Vercel (hosting) store and run AIMail on our behalf.
  • Nobody else. AIMail does not sell Google user data or share it for advertising.

Limited Use

AIMail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, AIMail:

  • uses Gmail data only to provide and improve AIMail's user-facing features;
  • does not transfer it to others except as needed to provide those features, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you;
  • does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train general AI models;
  • does not allow people to read it, unless you give permission for specific messages, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.
